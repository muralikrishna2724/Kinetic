Contrast

Body and muted text clear 4.5:1 against their own surface in both themes. Brand green is used for accents, icons and large text — never for body copy on a light background, where #00C878 on #FFFFFF sits below the AA threshold at small sizes. The dark theme brightens brand to #17E48F so it holds up on near-black.
